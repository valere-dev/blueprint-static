# Ad DNA — reading an ad as 20 genes

Source: `adn.machineroom.app` (`/srv/adn/index.html` mind map + lab, `simple.html` Evolve × Crafter
view, `script.html` pipeline, `builder.html` spec, `brief.html` clone a winner). This file is the
operational summary for analysis work: how to code an ad, how to count what is really new, how to plan
crosses, and how the simplified (course-aligned) view maps onto it.

> An ad isn't "a video". It's a combination of genes. Name every gene and you can count ads
> scientifically, know exactly what won, and spin a winner by swapping one piece at a time.

---

## 1. The four branches

| Branch | Genes | Rule | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | PD Product · OF Offer · DS Destination | Change one → **funnel test** (not a new creative) | Constants during a creative test |
| **Concept** (stage 1) | AV Avatar · AN Angle · ME Mechanism · ST Story · AW Awareness | Change one → **new concept (C)** | What we say, to whom |
| **Execution** (stage 2) | HK Hook · MS Messenger · FO Format · PR Proof · SC Script structure · TO Tone | Change one → **variation (V)** | How we say it |
| **Finish** (stage 3) | SE Setting · ED Editing · SO Sound · LE Length · CT CTA · CP Ad copy | Change one → **iteration (i)** | The polish |

Rule: **the highest branch that changed decides what you count.**

## 2. Gene catalogue (codes = position in the list, e.g. AN3 = Looks)

### Concept genes
**AV Avatar** — who exactly, by situation (trigger, cause, what they tried). Never demographics; age
and sex are chosen later through the actor.
AV1 Performance-driven (gym, work) · AV2 Hides it (avoids beach, shirt off, bedroom) · AV3 After a life
event (40, baby, menopause) · AV4 Just got a scary number / diagnosis · AV5 After another treatment
(GLP-1, pills) · AV6 Partner complains / is affected · AV7 Tried everything, now skeptical · AV8 Buying
for a loved one.
Cross: keep angle + mechanism, change who we talk to (words, setting, a messenger who looks like
them) → open a new audience with a proven concept.

**AN Angle** — the exact pain or desire attacked, the entry door.
AN1 Symptom (fatigue, sleep, belly, hair) · AN2 Performance (gym, sex, work) · AN3 Looks (mirror, man
boobs, skin) · AN4 Couple & family · AN5 Fear for future health · AN6 Identity ("be the man I used to
be") · AN7 Trigger event (40, summer, wedding) · AN8 Past failure ("nothing worked").
Cross: a mechanism won → it unlocks other pains (parasites → man boobs becomes parasites → erections,
hair, focus).

**ME Mechanism** — why the problem exists and why this fixes it when everything else failed.
ME1 Hidden cause inside the body (parasites, inflammation, cortisol) · ME2 Outside culprit (plastics,
modern food, industry) · ME3 Deficiency ("you're missing X") · ME4 Absorption ("the rest never gets
absorbed") · ME5 Hero ingredient (ancestral plant, clinical dose) · ME6 Timing or ritual · ME7 Synergy
(the combo that unlocks it) · ME8 New discovery (recent study).
Cross: the angle works but the mechanism is worn out or the market is saturated (erections: parasites
→ plastics → zinc deficiency).

**ST Story** — the big idea that wraps the mechanism.
ST1 Discovery ("a researcher found…") · ST2 Enemy / "you've been lied to" · ST3 Personal
transformation · ST4 Ancestral or cultural secret · ST5 Us vs them · ST6 Myth-busting ("stop doing X")
· ST7 News, something new · ST8 Pure education · ST9 Tested challenge ("30 days with…").
Cross: the concept is good but the ad is fatiguing ("you've been lied to" → "I tried it for 30 days").

**AW Awareness** — what the prospect already knows; decides where the ad starts.
AW1 Unaware · AW2 Problem-aware · AW3 Solution-aware · AW4 Product-aware · AW5 Ready to buy.
(Note: the Copy Constitution numbers awareness the other way round — AW5 unaware → AW1 most aware.
Always write the word, not only the code.)

### Execution genes
**HK Hook** — what stops the thumb; 3 layers you can swap separately: visual, on-screen text, voice.
HK1 Callout ("if you're over 40 and…") · HK2 Question · HK3 Bold contrarian claim · HK4 Number or stat
· HK5 Story open ("6 months ago…") · HK6 Scroll-stopping visual · HK7 Result first (before/after) · HK8
Secret, curiosity · HK9 Forbidden ("stop doing…") · HK10 Someone's reaction.
Cross: first move on any winner, the cheapest: 5–10 hooks shot in one session on the same body.

**MS Messenger** — who talks.
MS1 Regular customer (UGC) · MS2 Expert (doctor, naturopath, coach) · MS3 Founder · MS4 A loved one
(wife, daughter) · MS5 Known creator · MS6 Voice-over, no face · MS7 Older, wise character · MS8 AI
avatar / animation · MS9 Interview duo.
Cross: the script is good, credibility caps out (customer → doctor → his wife).

**FO Format** — the container.
FO1 Talking head · FO2 Podcast clip · FO3 Street interview · FO4 Green screen (article/study behind)
· FO5 Fake news report · FO6 Demo, tutorial · FO7 Routine, unboxing · FO8 Static image · FO9 Carousel
· FO10 Native screenshot (tweet, iPhone note, SMS, Reddit) · FO11 Before/after split · FO12 Long video
(VSL 2–5 min).
Cross: one winning script = several free formats (talking head → static → tweet → podcast).
(Compliance: "fake news report" formats imitating real media are banned for our brands — see
copy-analysis.md.)

**PR Proof** — the main proof on screen.
PR1 Testimonial · PR2 Study, scientific number · PR3 Visible demo · PR4 Before/after · PR5 Authority
· PR6 Volume (50,000 customers, 4.8★) · PR7 Guarantee, zero risk · PR8 Logic (the explanation is
enough).
Cross: people watch but don't buy.

**SC Script structure** — the order.
SC1 Problem → agitate → solve · SC2 Story (before → turning point → after) · SC3 List ("3 reasons") ·
SC4 Objection → answer · SC5 Side-by-side comparison · SC6 Education, product revealed late · SC7
Direct offer from second one.
Cross: watch time drops in the middle.

**TO Tone** — the dominant emotion.
TO1 Serious, medical · TO2 Humour · TO3 Emotional, vulnerable · TO4 Anger, outrage · TO5 Urgency, fear
· TO6 Buddy, casual · TO7 Aspirational, premium.
Cross: the audience is tired of one register.

### Foundation genes (frozen during a creative test)
**PD Product** (formula, form, packaging) — the "brand swipe cross": take a competitor's full structure
and execution, swap their product and mechanism for ours (e.g. a Mars Men winning format with our
angle + mechanism).
**OF Offer** (price, bundle, subscription, guarantee, gift) — test when conversion, not the click, is
the problem.
**DS Destination** (product page, advertorial, quiz, VSL, listicle) — test when CTR is good and
conversion isn't.

### Finish genes
SE Setting (car, kitchen, bathroom, doctor's office, gym, bed, outdoors) · ED Editing (pace, b-roll,
zooms, caption style) · SO Sound (music, voice, SFX, silence) · LE Length (15/30/60 s) · CT CTA ("tap
below", "take the quiz", promo) · CP Ad copy (primary text, headline, description).
Use when the ad fatigues (CPM up, CTR down): new setting + new edit on the same script = same ad, longer
life.

## 3. Counting and naming

| What changed | You count | Code | For Meta |
|---|---|---|---|
| Any Concept gene | New concept | C+1 | Truly new: can reach new people |
| Only Execution genes | New variation of the same concept | V+1 | Close: may be grouped with the original |
| Only Finish genes | Iteration (same ad) | i+1 | Near-identical: zero diversity |
| A Foundation gene | Funnel test, not a new creative | — | Test separately, creative frozen |

**Ad name:** `BRAND-C014-V03-i2` (brand · concept · variation · iteration).
**DNA string:** `AV2·AN3·ME1·ST2·AW2 | HK1·MS2·FO4·PR2·SC1·TO1`.

**The number that matters is concepts, not ads.** 60 ads that differ only by hook = 1 concept,
60 variations. Meta sees them as nearly the same ad: they steal delivery from each other instead of
reaching new people. A healthy account runs 3–5 live concepts at once.

**Two modes, never mixed.**
- Extending a winner → change **1 gene** (otherwise you don't know what worked).
- Hunting a new winner → change **at least 2 Concept genes** (otherwise Meta sees the same ad).

Vocabulary warning: in Ad DNA, "concept" = the strategy genes (who × which pain × which mechanism).
In Evolve, "concept" = the idea being tested ("test us-vs-them"). Say which one you mean.

| PROOF | Study on green screen, diagram of the gut. |

|---|---|---|---|
| Angle | AN3 → AN2 | Same parasites → fat → estrogen, pain = erections failing | C002 |
| Angle | AN3 → AN1 | Same mechanism, pain = hair loss | C003 |
| Mechanism | ME1 → ME2 | Same man boobs, cause = plastics in water and food | C004 |

## 5. Test order (go down a branch only once the branch above has a winner)

1. **Find the concept** — 3–5 concepts with different Angle × Mechanism. Execution deliberately
   identical (same simple format, same messenger) so only the concept varies.
2. **Find the hook** — on the winning concept, 5–10 hooks on the same body. Cheapest cross, biggest
   effect on watch rate.
3. **Find the mouth and the container** — messenger, format and proof crosses. Keep the best hook.
4. **Make it last** — finish iterations when the ad fatigues (CPM up, CTR down).
5. **Back to the top** — angle crosses on the winning mechanism, then mechanism crosses on the
   winning angle. Every validated mechanism unlocks a family of angles.

The lab (on the site) computes the verdict against a reference: NEW CONCEPT (a concept gene changed;
"clean cross" if exactly one), VARIATION (only execution genes; warning if > 1 changed), SAME. The
combination space is huge (thousands of concepts × thousands of executions) — that is why you test
branch by branch, never all at once.

## 6. The simplified, course-aligned view (/simple) — use this with the team

Evolve's chain: **Product (UMP + UMS) → Desire (13) → Sophistication (1–5) → Sub-avatar (by
exclusion) → Angle (the outcome) → Ads.** An ad = **STRATEGY × SCRIPT × VISUAL**; script and visual
are independent axes.

| Family | Levers | Change → |
|---|---|---|
| STRATEGY (who, which desire, why it works) | Desire · Sub-avatar · Angle · Mechanism · Authority | **NEW** |
| SCRIPT (the words) | Hook (0–3 s, ×3) · Bridge (~8 s, ×3) · Hold (~32 s, ×1) | ITER, same angle |
| VISUAL (who, where, how it's cut) | Format · Actor · Location · Length · Edit | ITER, same words |

Fixed during a creative test: product, offer, landing page. Polish (not a lever): caption colour, "Shop
now", badges, logo, music track.

**Named moves on a winner**

| Move | Keeps | Changes | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Static ↔ video | The whole message, headline included | The medium | A winning angle must exist in both |
| Same script, new visual | Every word | Actor, location, format or edit (one) | Winner fatigues; keep hook wording ~80 % |
| Same visual, new words | The visual | Hook or bridge wording | Sniper / Evolve axis 6 |
| Answer the comment | Format, persona | The objection the hold answers (most-liked comment) | A recurring doubt blocks a pocket |
| Fill the gap | A format already winning | The angle, from another winner | Winning format A and winning angle B never combined |
| Isolate the sub-angle | Everything else | The angle cut down to one part | A winner mixes 2 ideas: which one sells? |
| Language / market | The message | Language + settings | A winner in one country |
| Other product | The whole creative | The SKU | Fits another product in the store |
| Wording cascade | Every past winner | Wording, replaced by a new golden nugget | A new review phrase works |
| Funnel stage | Angle | Length and offer (shorter, product-first, promo) | BOFU version of a TOFU winner |

**Script × visual grid:** one angle = a grid. Rows = script versions (hooks/bridges), columns = visual
versions (actor/location/format). Along a row = same words, new visual (visual ITER); down a column =
same visual, new words (script ITER). Never change both at once.

**What to change, for the result you see**

| What you see | The problem | Pull first | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doesn't spend, bad CPA, nobody stops | The hook doesn't reach the right people | HOOK (more specific, not louder) | Format, actor |
| They stop, then drop | The middle loses them | BRIDGE | Edit pace, length |
| They watch, don't click | Not believable / no reason to act | HOLD (proof, objection, booster) | Authority, mechanism |
| They click, don't buy | The page doesn't continue the ad | LANDING / OFFER | Page adapted to the winning angle (week 3) |
| A top comment keeps coming back | A pocket blocked by an objection | Answer the comment | One ad per recurring objection |
| Winner fatigues | Same people saw it too often | Actor, location, format (same words) | Static ↔ video |
| Well-researched angle flops | Execution, not the angle | Hook, format | Drop the angle only after 20–30 creatives |
| Can't scale past a budget | Angle audience used up | ANGLE (Marksman) | Sub-avatar, language/market |
| Everyone in the market says the same | Sophistication 3–5 | SUB-AVATAR (exclusion resets) | Mechanism (new or better) |

**Known defects of the detailed tabs** (kept as built): demographic avatars there are an Evolve error;
hook and format sit at the same "variation" level though script and visual are separate axes;
"concept" means strategy there. When in doubt, follow /simple.

## 7. The clone system on top of the DNA (current house method)

Since the founder rejected the "pick 6 genes + 80/20 iterations" model, ads are made by **transplanting
a winner**. In DNA terms:
- The source ad's **Execution + Finish genes are kept** (format, structure, messenger type, tone,
  length, shot grammar) and its **awareness level** is kept.
- Only **AV and AN** are chosen (avatar first, then angle); **ME follows** from the mechanism → angles
  map; **PD** becomes ours. This is a "brand swipe cross" + an angle cross.
- The remix tool idea (not built): drop a winning video → frames + transcript → decode in ~5 points →
  4 big buttons **Hook · Avatar · Angle · Format** (+ "more": actor, length, edit, remove) → new
  script + editor notes. No Product button: the product stays the same.

Clone testing: one ad set = one source, 3-2-2 shell (3 creatives × 2 primary texts × 2 headlines); the
ad name carries source + angle; a winning clone becomes a source itself; a source that loses on 3
angles is dropped; an angle that loses on 3 sources is dropped.

## 8. Script pipeline (7 moments) — for reading video ads

Every video beat belongs to one of 7 colour-coded moments: **Hook** (0–3 s) · **Problem** ·
**Mechanism** · **Product** (~25–33 s) · **Proof** (~33–40 s) · **Offer** (~40–45 s) · **CTA**
(~45–48 s). Gene → moment: AV/AN live in Hook + Problem; ME in Mechanism + Product; PR in Proof; OF in
Offer; DS where the CTA sends; ST, AW, MS, FO, SC, TO, SE, ED, SO span the whole ad.

Ownership: Strategy (AV, AN, ME, ST, AW, PD, OF) → Script (HK, PR, SC, TO, CT) → Shoot (MS, FO, SE) →
Edit (ED, SO, LE) → Launch (CP, DS). The script decides WHAT (words, order, who speaks, proof, visual
direction); the editor decides HOW (b-roll, captions, music, SFX, pace, exports) and never changes words
or claims.

## 9. Scoring an ad with the DNA (analysis checklist)

For any ad under review, write:
1. DNA string (all 11 coded genes) + foundation (product, offer, destination).
2. Which gene most likely carries the win (usually AN or HK for statics; AN + ST + MS for video).
   Ads Brain rule: "what is the ONE thing that made this win?" — answer before producing anything.
3. Whether the competitor is extending a winner (1-gene changes across their variants) or hunting
   (≥ 2 concept genes). Their pattern of variants tells you which gene they believe in.
4. The 3 cheapest crosses for us (usually: angle cross on their mechanism, avatar cross, hook cross).
5. The family count: how many distinct CONCEPTS the competitor runs (not ads). A brand running 400 ads
   on 3 concepts is extending; one running 40 ads on 25 concepts is hunting.
