# Competitor Case Studies — what we already know (reuse before paying for new data)

Distilled findings of every competitor study done so far (Sept 2026). Markets: men's testosterone /
organ supplements (US), plus one multi-product health advertiser (Resilia) and the scaling-shops scan.
Each case lists: product · offer · what wins · the mechanism → angle pattern · routing · the lesson that
transfers to ANY brand.

Live sites (English, noindex): `intel.machineroom.app` (Meta library study: Elora, Primal Edge, Primal
Viking, Mars Men; `/en/primaledge`), `angles.machineroom.app` (statics study: Primal Viking, Mars Men,
Ancestral, Primal Storm; `/focus` = cortisol · libido · fat & aromatase), `quizmap.machineroom.app`,
`radar.machineroom.app` (Resilia), `scaling.machineroom.app`, `testo-map` (desires & avatars, July),
`offers.machineroom.app` (offers and gifts of ~120 brands), `brands.machineroom.app` (Meta library links).
Data: `/root/workspace/competitors/` (adlib, angles-site, *-statics, quiz-map, offers),
`/srv/intel/data/*.json`, `/root/workspace/radar/`, `/root/workspace/scaling/`.

Budget shares below are **estimates** (rank-weighted on Meta's impressions order, weight 1/rank^0.8).

## 1. Primal Edge Bison (buy-primaledge.com) — the reference competitor for organ capsules

- **Product**: 4 wild bison organs (liver, kidney, heart, testicle), 60 capsules, "Natural Testosterone
  Support". Same product Wildhorn sells under another brand.
- **Offer**: $49.95/month subscription (crossed $100); $75 one-time; Buy 2 Get 1, Buy 3 Get 2; 60-day
  "flat gut or your money back"; 50,000+ men, 4.8/5, "sold out 12 times last year". Stack: Shopify +
  GemPages advertorials + Kaching Bundles.
- **Library** (100 % of ads): 573 ads, 214 active, 62 videos, 22 landing pages. In July: 45 of 46
  active ads were IMAGES — statics carry the account. Peak 262 live ads (April) → ~46 (July); traffic
  26.5k/month, −21 % in 30 days, 76 % US; audience 98 % men, 35–44 > 45–54 > 25–34 > 55–64.
- **Estimated angle share**: man boobs ≈ 58 % · belly ≈ 27 % · comparison ≈ 10 % · libido ≈ 4 %.
  **Mechanism**: aromatase/estrogen ≈ 77 % (cortisol, absorption, deficiency marginal).
- **The thesis that carries everything**: "A beer belly isn't a fat problem. It's an estrogen problem."
  4 organs = 4 jobs (blocker, regulator, burner, source). Anti-category: "It's a FOOD problem."
- **Top statics now**: "12 YEARS OF THIS?? GONE IN 21 DAYS" (7 variants) · "FLAT CHEST IN 3 WEEKS. [50,000
  Men Did It.]" · "COUNT THE ANIMALS." · "Reindeer. Bison." · "DOES YOUR CHEST STILL LOOK LIKE THIS?" ·
  "REINDEER VS BISON" · authenticity UGC "These two bottles look almost identical".
- **Most duplicated ad ever**: "I Tested 3 Organ Supplements For My Beer Belly" (13 copies), run through a
  persona page ("Mark Wilson").
- **Routing**: /pages/bb-lp advertorial "I Tested the 2 Top-Selling Organ Supplements… Only One Actually
  Worked" (76 ads) · man-boobs PDP "For Men With Man Boobs Who've Tried Everything" (87–100 ads, incl. a
  UK variant with Highland cattle) · /products/beer-belly "For Men With a Belly That Won't Budge" ·
  listicles "10 Reasons Men 50-58+ Are Finally Losing the Man Boobs", "10 Things No One Tells Men Over 45
  About Man Boobs" · cortisol advertorial "The Belly That Won't Budge Is NOT Fat. It's Cortisol." ·
  libido PDP "…I STILL Couldn't Stay Hard When She Reached For Me." · advertorial "You Haven't Taken Your
  Shirt Off in Front of Anyone in How Long?".
- **Formats that win**: us-vs-them table (most duplicated format) · anatomy/mechanism (torso cutaway,
  visceral MRI, magnifier on estrogen) · product hero + 4 glowing organs (black/gold) · raw UGC (medicine
  cabinet, sticky note "this one works", shelfie) · cinematic ranch · all-text pattern interrupt (apology
  letter, official notice) · patriotic promo. Video signature: bottles that TALK (beef vs bison sketch).
- **Weakness**: shrinking live ads and traffic; market possibly tiring; "the original vs the copycats"
  is open.
- **Transferable lesson**: one mechanism, pushed hard on 2 body-zone angles (chest, belly) with a
  dedicated PDP per angle, beats a spread of 10 angles. Persona PDP headlines ("For men with X who've
  tried everything") are a cheap congruence win.

## 2. Elora Goods (eloragoods.com) — a same-product challenger

- **Product/offer**: "American Primal Bison" capsules; $32 (vs $75), 3 bottles $60, 5 bottles $90; 90-day
  guarantee "chest back or every penny back"; 80,000+ men; a "doctor user" story.
- **Library**: 122 ads (121 active), 15 videos, 7 landing pages. Est. share: comparison ≈ 35 % · belly ≈
  29 % · man boobs ≈ 29 %. Mechanism: aromatase ≈ 56 %, deficiency ≈ 20 %.
- **Almost everything is a comparison chart**: "WANTED FOR CRIMES AGAINST AMERICAN MEN" (top, ≈ 12 %) ·
  "3 ORGAN SUPPLEMENTS. 1 BEER BELLY. WHICH ONE ACTUALLY WORKS?" · "READ THE LABELS. YOUR BODY ALREADY
  CAN." · "THE FRONT IS MARKETING. THE BACK IS TRUTH." · authenticity line "30 DAYS WASTED. DON'T DO WHAT
  I DID." / "$75 GONE." / "WATCH OUT FOR SCAMS." / "KNOW BEFORE YOU BUY." / "5 THINGS TO CHECK.".
- **Routing**: /pages/comparison "I Tested the 2 Top-Selling Organ Supplements…" (copied structure) ·
  advertorial "The Hormone Your Doctor Measured but Never Explained" · listicles "10 Reasons This Beer
  Belly Melter Is Trending for Men 45-65 in 2026", "…Finally Losing the Soft Chest (It Was Never Fat)" ·
  a quiz "Find Out What's Really Behind Your Belly, Energy, And Drive".
- **Lesson**: a late entrant in a category full of look-alikes turns "fakes and copycats" into its main
  angle (buy direct, read the label). When the product is commoditized, **authenticity + price** become
  angles of their own. Also: a challenger copies the leader's best advertorial structure 1:1 — the
  skeleton is the asset.

## 3. Primal Viking (primalviking.com) — reindeer organs, the widest angle spread

- **Product**: freeze-dried reindeer organs (liver, heart, kidney) + beef testicles + Arctic herbs
  (rhodiola, sea buckthorn, nettle), 48 capsules. **Offer**: "60 % / 63 % OFF" subscription — 1 jar
  $49.99 (was $125), 2 jars $45.99/jar (bestseller), 4 jars $37.49; refill every 48 days; 30-day
  guarantee; 19-minute countdown + "24 bottles left". 100,000+ / 149,214+ men.
- **Library** (top 250 impressions + 100 latest): est. share belly ≈ 47 % · man boobs ≈ 12 % · libido ≈
  11 % · size ≈ 9 %. Mechanism: deficiency ≈ 41 %, none ≈ 16 %, cortisol ≈ 14 %, blood flow ≈ 10 %, age
  decline ≈ 9 %.
- **Top creatives**: "THE 90-DAY CHALLENGE. MELT 27 LBS OR MONEY BACK." (≈ 9 %, 84 days) · "GOODBYE DAD
  BOD BELLY" (119 days) · "+1 INCH IN 30 DAYS OR YOUR MONEY BACK" (copied from Primal Storm) · "SUCKS OUT
  STUBBORN BEER BELLY IN WEEKS" · "DO NOT IGNORE THIS CHANGE" (156 days) · "DISSOLVES LOVE HANDLES &
  CORTISOL BELLY BY DAY 28". Statics winners stayed live 206–285 days; videos 151–264 days. Best video
  winners: "13 years of beer belly / man boobs gone in 9 days" and a celebrity strongman series.
- **The pattern**: one product, one big promise ("your belly, man boobs and fatigue are not age, they are
  your hormones"); **the villain changes, the solution never does** (testosterone, estrogen, cortisol,
  sugar, thyroid → reindeer organs). Bridge = Vikings ate reindeer heart, liver and testicles.
- **16 angles** in the statics study, incl. cortisol through DANDRUFF ("it was never the shampoo"), the
  wife POV ("my husband got soft, then I saved him"), "your labs are 'normal'. You're not.", GLP-1
  ("not one log, the whole fireplace"), joints → a second product (joint balm + cod liver oil bundle),
  health fear (what fat does to organs), anti-TRT.
- **Routing**: "one landing per villain" — sex/size → libido page; man boobs → man-boobs pages; belly →
  "10 reasons beer belly" → "7 reasons"; cortisol → "You're NOT Fat, You're Inflamed from High Cortisol";
  GLP-1 → its page; joints → the second product; everything general → the catch-all advertorial; the
  quiz (`quiz-reindeer`) receives the most ads (201).
- **Lesson**: a product with a vague mechanism ("deficiency") can carry many angles if each villain gets
  its own landing page; new angles are found in odd symptoms (dandruff) that the same villain explains.
  Watch for angles they copy from a smaller brand (size guarantee from Primal Storm).

## 4. Mars Men (mengotomars.com) — the proof-driven stack

- **Product**: "Boost" 11 ingredients with clinical doses on the label (tongkat 500 mg, shilajit 400 mg,
  cistanche, fenugreek, boron, zinc, vitamin D 4,000 IU…); new "Ignite" focus drink. **Offer**: "50 % OFF
  FOR LIFE" $59/month (was $118) + "$70 in free gifts", free shipping, 90-day "Higher T or your money
  back", HSA/FSA eligible; media-partner codes at 60 %.
- **Library** (top 150 + latest 100): est. share generic T ≈ 28 % · energy ≈ 14 % · GLP-1 ≈ 12 % ·
  generation ≈ 12 % · belly ≈ 11 % · man boobs ≈ 9 % · face ≈ 8 %. Mechanism: cortisol ≈ 26 %, endocrine
  disruptors ≈ 26 %, aromatase ≈ 16 %, none ≈ 16 %, SHBG ≈ 11 %.
- **Top creatives**: "50 % OFF FOR LIFE" (≈ 11 %, 39 variants; generational comparison with grandpa) ·
  "THIS DEAL WON'T LAST LONG" (152 days) · "BUILT FOR MEN WHO REFUSE TO SLOW DOWN" · "I quit TRT, started
  Mars Men. Got leaner than I ever did on injections." (149 days, 45 variants) · "Lose Fat, Not Strength."
  (GLP-1, 152 days). Oldest winner of all four brands: "We Made Too Much" (493 days).
- **Positioning**: enemy #1 = TRT ("Don't take T. Make T."), enemy #2 = underdosed proprietary blends.
  Proof = bloodwork numbers (219 → 499, 254 → 861), Trustpilot, doses on the label, a medical board
  (Oxford PhD). Tone: Mars/astronaut universe, masculine and funny. Lo-fi sticky-note statics among
  historical winners.
- **Routing**: one "10 reasons" listicle cloned per angle (weight loss, GLP-1, TRT, modern man); proof
  ads → proof page "THEY LAUGHED WHEN I DECLINED TRT, BUT WHEN THEY SAW MY LABS…"; quiz `quiz-v6` for cold
  audiences (10 questions, 5 hidden personas). Avoid copying: fake media partnerships (NY Post,
  Thrillist co-branded pages).
- **Current tests**: Ignite launch (11 of 30 recent statics), a cannabis hook ("SMOKE WEED EVERYDAY?") that
  sends to the generic T quiz.
- **Lesson**: in a level-4 market, proof of the INPUT (doses, bloodwork) and a clear enemy outside the
  category (TRT) carry a generic angle; the offer itself (reason-why discount) can be the longest-running
  winner.

## 5. Ancestral Supplements (ancestralsupplements.com) — the catalogue brand

- **Product range**: grass-fed beef organs from NZ/Australia — Beef Liver (hero), Thyroid, FEM (women),
  MOFO (men), Minerals, Digestive Enzymes. **Offer**: "Subscribe & Save 45 % for life" + stacked free
  gifts (tallow, minerals, magnesium spray, tote, e-book); "Buy 4 Get 2 FREE"; 60-day / 365-day (FEM)
  guarantees; 3 million+ customers, 32,000+ reviews.
- **Winners**: statics live 232–450 days; half of the winners are Christmas ads (caution: TrendTrack may
  have missed their end dates).
- **Pattern**: no single big angle; one product per need, one angle per product. Real engine = the OFFER
  ("THIS DEAL IS INSANE", stacked gifts with crossed-out values). Soft mechanism everywhere:
  "nose-to-tail wisdom" + "modern food leaves gaps" + "like supports like" + quality (freeze-dried,
  third-party tested).
- **Most copyable format**: Instagram-story UGC (a hand holding the bottle + 3 testimonial bubbles).
- **Shift**: women 30–50 (PMS, perimenopause, postpartum: 13 of 30 recent ads). MOFO for men: "Why Men
  Today Feel Half As Powerful As Their Fathers" → the "Masculine Energy Gap"; "Morning Wood Returns (Wife
  Approved)*".
- **Lesson**: a soft mechanism + a big perceived-value offer can sustain a catalogue for years; seasonal
  (holiday) creatives can be evergreen winners; "like supports like" is a reusable belief bridge for
  organ products.

## 6. Primal Storm (primal-storm.com) — the blunt one

- **Product**: 5 herbs with doses on the label (tongkat 400 mg 100:1, fadogia 300 mg, beetroot 500 mg,
  ginseng, piperine), 3 capsules/day; FlowRevive (prostate) given free. **Offer**: every 28 days, 1 month
  $49, "BUY 2 GET 1 FREE" $98 (most popular), 3+2 $147; free e-book; 60-day guarantee "less than 2 % ever
  claim it"; 8-minute countdown.
- **Winners**: young brand — statics 136–203 days.
- **Pattern**: the guarantee IS the headline ("+3 INCHES OR MONEY BACK", "SHE'LL FORGET HER VIBRATOR OR
  MONEY BACK"); forbidden things said through metaphors (banana, cucumber, hot dog, eggplant, melting
  popsicle) and mascots (the jar lifting weights) to pass moderation. Mechanism: "The Nigerian Protocol"
  (fadogia used "for generations"; size chart by country). Offer "Buy 2 Get 1 Free" remixed endlessly
  (3D mascots, birthday cake, shipping box: 9 of 29 winners).
- **September pivot**: "Prediabetes killed your erections, we'll fix it. Simple." (13 of 30 recent), with
  33-step prediabetes/diabetes quizzes ("your personal blood flow plan", A1C level) and the free prostate
  bundle; also in Spanish for US Hispanics. "Performance AND prostate: why choose?" (DHT/estrogen → T
  down AND prostate up — two products for one cause).
- **Lesson**: a narrow sub-avatar with a medical trigger (prediabetic men) resets sophistication for a
  saturated desire (erections); the quiz qualifies the trigger; a free second product turns one cause into
  two benefits. Metaphor objects are how sexual templates survive — decode them (template-reading §2.5).

## 8. Resilia (resilia.shop) — reading a multi-product advertiser with the radar

- 3 products: aged odorless garlic (heart/circulation), cinnamon (blood sugar), oil of oregano
  (gut/parasites/"feeling off"). 37 of 38 pages push a single product; pages are disguised as health
  review sites.
- Oregano = pillar: two oldest winners live 13 months ("Craving sweets all the time? Still bloated?",
  "Still feeling off no matter how clean you eat?"), most winners, dozens of launches a day through
  pre-landers (parasites, GLP-1, men 40+, prostate, gut reset).
- Garlic = pushed hardest now: launched in summer on "Take Control of Your Heart Health Naturally?",
  dozens of winners, zero cuts, ~9 disguised review pages.
- Cinnamon = in test: "Support Healthy Blood Sugar — All Day Long?", no winner yet, launch pace slowing,
  sent straight to the PDP (no advertorial).
- Cadence: 150–220 new creatives a day in waves; 1 in 4 cut creatives die in < 1 week; creatives that
  survive 2 weeks often last 1–2 months.
- **Lesson**: read a big advertiser per product: which product has the oldest winners (pillar), which has
  the most launches and no cuts (push), which goes direct to PDP without winners (test). Pre-landers and
  disguised pages per angle are where the angle work lives.

## 9. Quiz funnels (Mars Men vs Primal Viking)

- Mars Men: custom app, 10 linear one-tap questions (~90 s), no branch, verdict in 3 levels (nobody is
  healthy), named root causes, a projection chart "+60 % T by [date 3 months out]" that sells the 90-day
  supply, 5 hidden personas in the URL, no email asked (a gated version exists in code), long sales page
  after. 4 of 10 questions change nothing.
- Primal Viking: Shopify theme section, 18 questions (~3–4 min) with body pictures, height/weight, an event
  + date (the only branch → countdown "86 days away"), a T-score capped at 58 + "biological age", plan
  with dated milestones, then the PDP with 2 jars on subscription preselected; A/B test of a "SKIP QUIZ &
  BUY NOW" button (~50 %). 3 of 18 questions change nothing.
- Both ignore the answers on the landing page. Lessons in research-playbook.md §10.

## 10. Scaling shops scan (28 Sep)

Shops created 15 May–15 Jun 2026 with ≥ 10 active ads: 4,302 domains → 1,276 scaling (≥ +50 % active ads
in 4 weeks); 72 curated, 22 in health/supplements; dominant new health angle: lymphatic-drainage drops
(5 brands). Product watchlist (not launched without a go): astaxanthin 12 mg, kids' multivitamin +
omega-3, R-ALA 600 mg, taurine (pets), Ceylon cinnamon 7,200 mg, black seed oil… **Lesson**: when a new
product shows up in 5 young shops at once, the mechanism is fresh — the window for a level-1/2 claim is
short.

## 11. The Wildhorn worked example (the system applied to one brand)

- Research (12 Jul) on Primal Edge → 5 angles: **A1 beer belly = estrogen** (king) · **A2 man boobs /
  puffy nipples** · **A3 wild vs farmed** · **A4 "I tested 3 organ supplements"** · **A5 anti-waste ("I
  spent $1,750", "12 pills", "a FOOD problem")**. Secondary: day 3/7/14/30 timeline · ancestral/patriotic
  + holiday promos · scarcity/authenticity · cortisol belly + GLP-1 twist · "the doctor said it's age".
- Top 5 headlines: "BEER BELLY GONE IN 21 DAYS. ONLY ONE BLOCKS THE CONVERSION." · "MAN BOOBS? DON'T
  HIDE — MELT THEM." · "I Tested 3 Organ Supplements For My Beer Belly. Here's The Truth." · "FARMED
  REINDEER vs WILD AMERICAN BISON" · "Supplements failed you. This won't."
- Locked angle (26 Jul): "Your fat is not fat." (one angle = one landing page).
- Brand kit saved (1 Oct): research.md, angle-01.md, product.md, product photo, creatives.jsonl
  (`/root/workspace/wh-remix/kit/`). 79 templates sorted into 9 families, the 5 angles spread across each.
- Review rounds then produced the template-reading rules (0/79 → 6/10 → 26/34 first-try validation).
- Differentiation still open: bison, reindeer and beef sources are all "taken" — attack as "the original
  vs the copycats" (L4) or find a new proof/mechanism; guarantee and offer locked by the founder.
