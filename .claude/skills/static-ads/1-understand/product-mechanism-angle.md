# Product → Mechanism → Angle

The single most important analytical move in this system. A product is sold through a **mechanism**
(why the problem exists and why this fixes it). A mechanism opens a **family of angles** (the outcomes
people want). Ads are angles. So the order of thinking is always:

```
PRODUCT  ──►  MECHANISM(S)  ──►  ANGLES (outcomes)  ──►  ADS
 what it is     the hidden cause     what the customer       one ad = one angle,
 what it does   + why this fixes it  wants, in their words   one sub-avatar, one promise
```

When writing ads the order flips: **avatar → angle → (mechanism follows from the map)**. The map is
built once, here; the ad writer never invents a mechanism on the fly.

---

## 1. Definitions (house meaning)

| Term | Definition | Example (bison organ capsules) |
|---|---|---|
| Physical product | Form, ingredients, dose, packaging | 4 freeze-dried bison organs, 2 capsules/day |
| Functional product | What it does for the person | Supports healthy testosterone; belly and chest fat respond |
| UMP — unique mechanism of the problem | The hidden root cause + why everything tried failed | "After 40, belly fat converts testosterone into estrogen, which stores more fat. A loop. Diets fight the wrong enemy." |
| UMS — unique mechanism of the solution | Why THIS works, in "A + B = C" | "Kidney blocks the conversion + liver clears excess + heart burns the store + testicle feeds the source = the full system." |
| Enemy name | The on-air name of the UMP, 2–4 plain words | "the estrogen conversion" / "the belly loop" |
| Action name | The on-air name of the fix | "block the conversion" |
| Belief-bridge fact | A fact the avatar already accepts without proof, the first step of the chain | "Testosterone drops after 40." |
| Elimination triad | Not X, not Y, not Z | "Not your diet. Not your discipline. Not your age." |
| Angle | The outcome the customer wants, in their words | "Flat belly again" · "Shirt off at the pool" · "She notices" |

Rules for every mechanism:
- **Three-link causal chain, never an ingredient alone**: [ingredient] → [intermediate action] →
  [benefit the person FEELS]. Each link must be believable alone; if one needs a leap, add a link.
  Use explicit connectors ("that's why", "which means", "so").
- **Simplify for a 14-year-old.** No jargon to look expert. "Aromatase" may appear at most once on a
  landing page and never in an ad hook.
- **Name it.** A named mechanism is remembered, compared and asked for, and protects against clones.
- **The UMS goes in almost every ad. The UMP can be dropped lower in the funnel** (MOFU/BOFU).
- **Never contradict an existing belief head-on.** Start from it ("the ones you took were
  under-dosed"), then redefine the category against it.

## 2. Method: build the mechanism inventory for a new product

### 2.1 Collect candidate mechanisms (4 sources)
1. **The product's own page and label**: ingredients, dose, claims the brand is allowed to make.
2. **Competitors' ads and landing pages** (same product or same desire): every cause they accuse.
   Use the fixed list of "villains" seen in the testosterone market as a checklist for any category:
   hormonal conversion · stress hormone · nutrient deficiency of modern food · poor absorption /
   inflamed gut · endocrine disruptors / plastics · binding protein locking the hormone · blood flow /
   nitric oxide · precursors missing · natural decline with age · parasites / toxins · insulin / blood
   sugar · inflammation.
3. **Voice of customer**: what customers THINK is causing it ("I eat clean and still…", "my doctor said
   it's age"). Beliefs you can start from.
4. **The "hidden mechanism" prompt** (Evolve): "What does the product do that you don't advertise?
   What would customers complain about if it worked too well?" Plus: "Identify the new mechanisms that
   would resonate in [market]" with the product overview + URL loaded.

### 2.2 Fill a mechanism card for each candidate
```
Mechanism: [name] (enemy → fix)
- Enemy name said on air:
- Action name:
- Belief-bridge fact (accepted without proof) + source:
- Problem chain (3–5 links, plain words, source each):
- Solution in one sentence (A + B = C):
- Elimination triad (not X / not Y / not Z):
- Failed solutions it explains (name — why it misses this mechanism):
- Analogy a 14-year-old gets:
- Mechanism visual (3–5 step animation or static diagram):
- Forbidden terms + compliant rewrites:
- Freshness (how many shops scale on it?) / Belief (how plausible to the avatar?):
- Angles it can credibly reach:
```

### 2.3 Score Freshness × Belief (Crafter)
| | Low belief | High belief |
|---|---|---|
| **Fresh** (few shops scale on it) | Possible but hard: borrow authority (a known researcher, a culture, a study) | **Sweet spot** — lead with it |
| **Stale** (commodity, everyone says it) | Drop | High sophistication: niche the sub-avatar harder, or prove your version/format is better |

Check freshness on TrendTrack (how many advertisers scale on this cause in the last 60 days), Google
Trends, viral TikToks, Reddit volume. Check belief against the avatar's existing beliefs (the bridge).

### 2.4 Pick a differentiation case (Crafter's three)
1. **Format transplant** — same desire + same mechanism, better delivery (gummy vs pill, capsule vs
   powder). Easiest and lowest risk: you inherit competitors' education. The main battle in every ad
   is *why your format is better*; mine the negative reviews of the other format.
2. **Desire transplant** — same mechanism, new desire (collagen skin → hair). Medium effort. Change the
   UMP/UMS wording and **never mention the old desire's claim**.
3. **Core swap** — new mechanism for the same desire. You pay all the education from zero.

### 2.5 Draw the map
One line per mechanism, then a matrix.
```
Estrogen conversion (fat converts T → estrogen → more fat) :: flat belly | lose the man boobs | shirt off at the pool | drive back | "she notices"
Stress hormone (chronic cortisol = storage mode)        :: belly that won't move | sleep through the night | stop cravings after meals | calm at 6 p.m.
Modern-food deficiency (ancestral organs fill the gap)    :: energy back by 10 a.m. | strength back at the gym | feel like myself again
Wild vs farmed source (potency of the source animal)      :: (comparison / anti-waste angles) "only one worked" | "stop paying for 12 pills"
```

## 3. Method: spin N angles from ONE mechanism

A validated mechanism is worth more than a validated ad, because it unlocks a family of angles. To
spin angles:

1. Write the chain end to end, then list **every downstream symptom** the chain can honestly explain.
2. For each symptom, write the **outcome** the person wants (angle), the **scene** where they notice it,
   and **who else notices**.
3. Keep only symptoms that (a) the product can honour, (b) many people share, (c) are visible today
   (sell the repair, not the prevention).
4. Rank by observed market weight (competitor ads + VOC volume) and by distance from your current
   winning angle (closest first = cheapest test).

### Worked example 1 — Oil of oregano (the founder's own example)
Mechanism chain: gut parasites → nutrients poorly absorbed → the body stores fat → fat makes estrogen.

| Downstream symptom | Angle (outcome) | Scene | Who notices |
|---|---|---|---|
| Man boobs | "A flat chest again" | Shirt off at the beach | Kids, wife |
| Weak erections | "Hard again when she reaches for you" | Bedroom | Partner |
| Hair loss | "Stop finding hair on the pillow" | Shower drain, pillow | Barber |
| Belly | "Jeans that button again" | Getting dressed | Himself in the mirror |
| Brain fog / focus | "Clear head by 10 a.m." | Meeting at work | Boss, colleagues |
| Sugar cravings / bloating | "No more bloat after dinner" | After meals | Partner |

Second mechanism for the same product: **plastics/endocrine disruptors in water and food** → the same
man-boobs angle can be re-sold with a new cause (a Mechanism cross in Ad DNA = a new concept).
Third: **zinc deficiency** for erections. That is: one product, three mechanisms, each with its own
family of angles.

In-market confirmation (Resilia, radar study): oregano is their pillar product, sold through
pre-landers on parasites, GLP-1, men 40+, prostate and gut reset; their two oldest winners ran 13 months
on "Craving sweets all the time? Still bloated?" and "Still feeling off no matter how clean you eat?".
One mechanism (gut), many doors.

### Worked example 2 — Bison organ capsules (Wildhorn vs Primal Edge, same product)
Primal Edge's thesis carries everything: "A beer belly isn't a fat problem. It's an estrogen problem."
- UMP: an enzyme in belly fat converts testosterone into estrogen; estrogen orders more fat storage on
  belly and chest. A self-feeding loop. Soft conspiracy layer: "identified in 1979, doctors know".
- UMS (4 organs, 4 jobs): kidney = the blocker (selenium + zinc), liver = the regulator (vitamin A,
  B12, iron), heart = the burner (CoQ10 + carnitine), testicle = the source (androgen precursors).
- Anti-category reframe: "It's not a supplement problem. It's a FOOD problem." (14 carrots, 2 dozen
  oysters, 12 Brazil nuts, 9 ribeyes — or 2 capsules.)
- Angles that mechanism reaches: beer belly (king), man boobs / puffy nipples (strongest scroll-stop),
  drive in the bedroom, "feel like yourself again", shirt off at the pool.
- Separate mechanisms in the same market: **cortisol belly** (stress hormone; reaches cravings, sleep,
  puffy face, even dandruff at Primal Viking), **modern-food deficiency** (reaches energy, strength,
  "normal labs but not normal"), **source potency** (wild vs farmed: reaches comparison and anti-waste),
  **GLP-1 depletion** (reaches "lose fat, not strength").

Locked Wildhorn angle built from it: "Your fat is not fat." After 40 the body converts testosterone
into estrogen → stored on belly and chest. Diets and the gym fight the wrong enemy. 4 jobs in one jar.
Promise ladder: day 7 sleep, day 14 shirts, day 21 belt — or your money back.

### Worked example 3 — Testosterone stack (Mars Men)
Mechanisms seen in their ads, with the angle each one carries:
- Endocrine disruptors ("plastics, seed oils, blue light — your grandfather had more T at 60") →
  generation angle, generic T boost.
- Cortisol → belly, energy, GLP-1 ("lose fat, not strength").
- Aromatase + SHBG ("produce, free, protect, optimize") → man boobs/belly, anti-TRT, bloodwork proof.
Lesson: a stack product lets each ingredient carry its own mechanism ("tongkat blocks the enzyme that
steals T, boron frees bound T, shilajit supports production") — "one ingredient = one job" is itself a
proof device.

### Worked example 4 — Cinnamon / blood sugar (Resilia)
Mechanism: blood sugar swings. Angle tested: "Support healthy blood sugar — all day long?" Sent
straight to the product page, no advertorial; no winner after weeks. Read: a commodity mechanism with
no named enemy and no pre-sell page = high sophistication, low freshness. Fix candidates: niche the
sub-avatar (post-meal crash, prediabetic men worried about erections — Primal Storm moved hard to
"prediabetes killed your erections"), or name a fresher enemy.

## 4. Method: read the mechanism out of ANY ad (review mode)

For each ad, extract in this order:
1. **Symptom shown** (what the viewer recognises).
2. **Accused cause** (the villain). Classify into the fixed list (§2.1). "None" is a valid answer —
   many promo ads have none.
3. **Why prior solutions failed** (diet, gym, pills, TRT, the doctor).
4. **The fix** (how the product acts on the cause).
5. **The bridge** (the story or authority that makes the jump believable: Vikings, a ranch, a Nigerian
   protocol, a founder, a study).
6. **Angle** (the outcome promised).

Then produce the spin-off table:

| Product | Mechanism used | Angle used | Other angles on this mechanism | Other mechanisms for this product |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reindeer organs | Cortisol (storage mode) | Dandruff: "it was never the shampoo" | Belly, cravings after meals, puffy face, sleep | Deficiency, aromatase, blood flow |

This is the format to use for any batch of videos or ads someone sends "to review": not a summary —
an angle generator.

## 5. Pairing rule: the mechanism follows the angle

When an ad is being briefed:
1. The human (or the plan) picks the **sub-avatar**, then the **angle**.
2. Look the angle up in the map. The mechanism(s) listed against it are the only ones allowed.
3. If several mechanisms reach the angle, use the one closest to the "why" of the source ad being cloned.
4. If **none** reaches it: stop. Say "this product can't sell this angle" and propose the nearest
   angle that is on the map. Never improvise a link.

Between two angles on the same mechanism, only the language changes (the wanted result, the symptom,
what was tried, the final promise). The cause and the fix stay identical. The copy chain (Ad DNA
builder):
```
① result wanted, in the customer's words → ② symptom → ③ already tried → ④ hidden cause (UMP)
→ ⑤ what the product corrects (UMS) → ⑥ final promise that repeats ①
```
Prostate example: "sleep through the night" vs "sick of peeing all the time" — ①②③⑥ change,
④⑤ stay.

## 6. Mechanism quality checklist

- [ ] The enemy is named in 2–4 plain words with a physical verb (blocks, clears, locks, steals).
- [ ] The chain has 3–5 links, each believable alone, each sourced.
- [ ] It starts from a fact the avatar already believes.
- [ ] It explains why what they tried failed (permission: "it wasn't your fault").
- [ ] It makes the product the logical end of the chain (A + B = C).
- [ ] A 14-year-old can repeat it.
- [ ] It survives compliance: no disease claim, structure-function language ("supports", "helps
      maintain").
- [ ] Freshness × belief scored, differentiation case chosen.
- [ ] Angles listed, each with a scene and a witness.

## 7. Sophistication and the mechanism

| Market level | What the mechanism must do |
|---|---|
| 1 (nobody promises it) | Not needed. Plain claim. Don't over-sophisticate a virgin market. |
| 2 (a few promise it) | Bigger/faster claim, more proof. Mechanism optional. |
| 3 (everyone promises, nobody believes) | New, NAMED mechanism in the hook. |
| 4 (mechanisms copied) | Better mechanism: simpler, faster, more complete, no side effect. Demolish the others. |
| 5 (nothing believed) | Identity / community / story. |

Supplements in 2026 (sleep, testosterone, hair, blood sugar, menopause, libido, joints): assume level
3–4 by default. **Niching resets the level**: "weight loss" is 4–5, "arm fat after menopause" is lower,
"…in women who already tried GLP-1" can be 1–2. The cheapest way out of a saturated market is not a new
mechanism, it is a narrower sub-avatar. Escalate the mechanism, never the result number.
