# Edenvia avatar sheets (one per angle)

Converted 1:1 from the founder's ODT files (Drive folder *avatars*, 2026-10-11). Each sheet follows the same
structure: Step 1 Avatar (identity, awareness, current belief, pain, failed solutions, desired transformation,
objections, trigger moment, avatar language) → Step 2 Root-cause bridge → Step 4 Why everything else failed →
Step 5 Connect the product → Step 6 Big Idea → Step 7 Creative execution (10 hooks, opening, analogies) →
Step 8 Evidence required → Final summary.

Lines tagged `[ads]` come from a competitor's ads (Rovina); lines tagged `[inferred]` are the strategist's inference.
Steps 2–6 are written under a **counterfactual assumption** ("assume strong product-specific evidence proves…"):
they are angle strategy, **not** approved claims. Only the facts in `../brand.json` may be printed in an ad.
