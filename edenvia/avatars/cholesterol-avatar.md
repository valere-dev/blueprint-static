Per the supplied framework, mechanism selection comes before counterfactual amplification, and factual reality must remain separate from the hypothetical marketing world. Pasted markdown

Edenvia's strongest documented product mechanism is molecular hydrogen supporting the body's defenses against oxidative stress. The page also gives us two strategically important product properties: molecular hydrogen's extremely small molecular size and the claimed generation of 8 PPM fresh molecular hydrogen per tablet.

For this cholesterol avatar, there is a legitimate biological bridge, but it is important to define it correctly.

After a fat-containing meal, triglyceride-rich lipoproteins rise in the bloodstream. Excessive postprandial lipemia is associated with oxidative stress, inflammation, remnant lipoprotein accumulation, and endothelial dysfunction. Importantly, the evidence does not establish the simplistic claim that oxidative stress is always the primary reason triglycerides fail to clear. Postprandial triglyceride clearance also depends on lipoprotein production, lipoprotein lipase activity, insulin resistance, genetics, obesity, and other factors. PubMed

There is also relevant human research on hydrogen-rich water. A randomized trial in untreated hypercholesterolemia reported improvements in several measures of HDL function, protection against LDL oxidation, and a greater proportion of participants showing reductions in total and LDL cholesterol. Other trials have reported changes in cholesterol, redox biomarkers, and endothelial function. These studies involved hydrogen-rich water protocols, not Edenvia specifically, so they are category-level evidence, not finished-product proof. PubMed

There is therefore enough biological alignment to build a strong cholesterol world around Edenvia's flagship mechanism, but not enough evidence to presently claim Edenvia treats hypercholesterolemia, clears triglycerides overnight, removes plaque, or prevents cardiovascular disease.
