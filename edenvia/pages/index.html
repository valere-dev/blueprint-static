How a batch runs

  1. One family at a time. Gate 2 shows every template of the family with its trick and the Edenvia plan before anything is generated.
  2. Same amount of text as the template (R12), headlines from the research's bank (R9), the customer's problem before the product (R13).
  3. Real product only. The pouch comes from the brand photo library and is pasted by script, never redrawn (C23); the offer slot keeps parity with the template (B23).
  4. Review by Loom, 10 at a time. Each comment becomes a permanent rule; validated pixels are frozen (D21).
  5. Nothing invented. A fact missing from the brand kit stays out of the ad until it is real (B20).
