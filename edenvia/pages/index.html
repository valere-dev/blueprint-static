The rules that never regress

  1. Comparison templates: the losing side shows a generic rival and what she suffers, Edenvia and what she wants on the winning side. Never Edenvia on both sides.
  2. Real product only: the pouch is the studio photo lit by the scene, the logo is the official file. Never a flat cutout, never an invented badge.
  3. Every text line is rewritten for the avatar: no template line survives, no word from another niche. Sell, don't describe.
  4. Offers only from the allowed prices; a % stays a % ("-50%", not "$39"); a discount code becomes a discount, a price or a benefit.
  5. Ingredients: hydrogen, magnesium, minerals. Nothing else.
  6. Rival naming: "Average + angle + form" ("AVERAGE DEBLOAT PILLS"). Never a competitor name, a disease or a drug name.
  7. Every complaint becomes a rule in the brief or in the generator's prompts, and validated pixels are frozen.
